# The 20-Minute Pilot Conversation

A script for the live demo with one guar exporter. Goal: not to "wow"
them — to earn trust by being the first person who *didn't* oversell,
then show a rupee number on **their** volume.

---

### 0–2 min · The hook (one sentence, then stop)

> "You make two money decisions on every consignment — *when* to sell
> and *where* to sell — and right now you make both on feel. I built
> something that does both on data, in rupees, and I'll show you exactly
> what it can't do too."

### 2–6 min · The painful truth (let them nod)

- ~35% of India's guar goes to the US. The US is now tariff-stressed.
  "How much of *your* book is US?" — write it down, you'll use it.
- Guar price swings 30–100% in a year on US fracking demand. "When you
  locked your last annual contract — was that a good month or a bad
  one? How did you decide?" (Answer is always "experience / broker.")

### 6–10 min · The trust move (this is the whole pitch)

> "First, what this does NOT do. I tried to predict the guar price. I
> backtested it honestly. It does **not** beat just assuming next month
> equals this month. So I will never sell you a price forecast."

Show the honest banner on screen. Then:

> "What US drilling *can* tell you is **risk**. When drilling falls hard,
> guar demand is cooling and your price downside is elevated — that's
> when locking a forward protects you. That's the trigger."

### 10–15 min · Their numbers, live

Put **their** tonnage and US-share into the sidebar. Read the screen
back to them in their words:

- "On your ~__ t/yr, the downside you're carrying unhedged is **₹__**.
  Locking a forward when the trigger fires caps that."
- "You realise ~$1.66/kg into the US. Japan pays $2.71 — and India
  already ships there, so the channel exists. Shifting 20% of your US
  volume ≈ **₹__/yr** more. Here are the next three, ranked."

Show the SURGING/FADING radar: "These markets turned in the last 12
months. This is the part you'd re-check every month."

### 15–18 min · Where the price spine flags a repaired month

Point at the red ✕ (Oct-2021): "Our source mis-reported that month —
$7/kg, fake. We flag and fix it in the open and keep the raw value.
That's how everything in here is built. Nothing hidden."

### 18–20 min · The ask

> "Two things. One: is any of this wrong about your business? Two: would
> catching one bad lock, or one re-route, a year be worth a small fixed
> fee? If yes, I'll show you pricing. If the numbers don't move you,
> we shake hands and I've wasted only 20 minutes of yours."

---

## Objections — answer honestly, never dodge

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "Volza/Zauba already give me trade data." | "They sell *data*. This sells a *decision* with a rupee tag, on a driver (US rig count) they don't track. Different product." |
| "Comtrade is 6–18 months late." | "Correct — and stated on screen. WHEN runs on rig count, which is *weekly* and ahead of the price. WHERE is structural (who pays more changes slowly). Neither needs last week's data." |
| "Can't you just predict the price?" | "No, and I proved it to you rather than pretend. Anyone who says they can is selling you something." |
| "Why Japan/Oman — I don't ship there." | "India does, materially — so the channel and grade-fit are proven and switching friction is low. We only suggest markets with a real existing India→there flow." |
| "Your sample numbers aren't mine." | "Right — that's why this is a pilot. Give me your real tonnage and mix; the screen recomputes to you in 10 seconds." |
