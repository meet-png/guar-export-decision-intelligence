# Pricing — One Pager

The whole pricing argument is one ratio: **what one good decision is
worth vs what the tool costs.**

---

## The value anchor (sample 600 t/yr exporter)

- One mistimed annual lock, or staying over-exposed to a fading market,
  is a **₹ tens-of-lakhs** event (the sample shows ~₹87.7 L downside
  carried, ~₹47 L/yr left on the table by not re-routing).
- Against that, a software fee is a rounding error. We price so that
  **catching one decision a year pays for several years of the tool.**

## The offer

| Tier | Price | For whom |
|---|---|---|
| **Pilot** (first user) | **Free for 3 months** | In exchange for: your real tonnage/mix for the demo, monthly feedback, and — if it delivers — a reference call. |
| **Standard** | **₹15,000 / month** (₹1.5 L/yr) | A single guar exporter/processor. Monthly refreshed WHEN trigger + WHERE radar + your ₹ dashboard. |
| **Pilot → Standard** | 50% off year one | If the pilot converts. |

₹1.5 L/yr against a ₹40–90 L decision surface is a **<3%** insurance
premium on the part of the business you currently run on feel.

## What you get / don't get — stated plainly

**Get:** the one-screen dashboard on your numbers; the monthly WHEN
trigger and WHERE radar; the honest backtest and every assumption shown;
limitations disclosed in writing.

**Don't get (by design):** a price forecast (we proved we can't, and
won't fake it); investment advice; any guarantee of a market outcome.
This is decision-framing on public data — your judgment still makes the
call.

## Why this price is honest, not anchored-high

It is deliberately *below* the value so the decision is obvious, and
*above* free so it's taken seriously and funds upkeep (weekly data
refresh, FX/limits review). Cancel any month. No lock-in — the only
lock-in we believe in is the forward contract the tool tells you to sign.
