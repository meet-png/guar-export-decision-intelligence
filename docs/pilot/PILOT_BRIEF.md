## You make two money decisions on every shipment. Today, both on gut.

**WHEN to sell** (sell now, or lock a forward contract) and **WHERE to
sell** (which market). This tool answers both, on public data, in your
rupees.

## What it is worth (sample: 600 t/yr, 45% to the US)

| | What it does | On the sample volume |
|---|---|---|
| **WHEN** | Flags when US drilling (guar's swing demand) signals elevated price **downside** — lock a forward to cap it | **≈ ₹87.7 lakh/yr** of downside at risk, capped by acting |
| **WHERE** | Ranks which markets pay more per kg and are growing; you're **34.6%** exposed to the now-tariffed US | Re-routing **20%** of US volume to **Japan** ($2.71 vs $1.66/kg) **≈ ₹47 lakh/yr** more |

## What it deliberately does **not** do

It does **not** predict the guar price. We backtested that honestly —
the model does **not** beat "next month = this month" (MAPE 3.6% vs
3.5%; direction-hit 58%→32%). We tell you that up front. A tool that
oversells a forecast is a tool that loses you money. WHEN is a **risk
trigger**, not a crystal ball — that honesty is the point.

## What it runs on

Free public data only: UN Comtrade (India's guar exports, HS 130232,
2019–2024), Baker Hughes US rig count, IMD Rajasthan monsoon. Every
assumption is shown. Known limits stated, not hidden: trade data lags
6–18 months (decision-framing, not booked); market view is India-side;
FX (₹83/$) is explicit and the figures scale linearly with it.

## The pilot ask

20 minutes. You give us your real annual tonnage and rough destination
mix. We show **your** WHEN trigger, **your** top-paying markets, and
**your** rupee numbers — live, on screen. No data of yours leaves the
room; nothing is shared or stored without your say-so.

> If after that the numbers aren't worth a fraction of one good decision
> a year, there's nothing to discuss. If they are, we talk pricing.
